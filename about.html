WHY WE EXIST

The most interesting opportunities are rarely labelled real estate.

A parcel in Dubai South is not only land. It is an aviation corridor, a power allocation, a planning envelope and a capital structure. A data centre is not only a building. It is a grid connection, a fibre route, a cooling strategy and a tenant covenant.

The assets that matter now sit across categories — and the advice around them has to do the same. BizMaker was founded to bring those perspectives to one table.

Our advisory, capital and infrastructure practices share a single mandate: understand the asset in full before anyone commits capital to it.

02 — PRINCIPLES

What we believe. How we work.

Four principles shape every mandate — from a first call to the final handover. They are deliberately practical.

01

Asset before narrative

A compelling story is not a valuation. We start with location, planning, income, cost and exit — the evidence beneath the pitch.

02

Capital has a shape

Debt, equity, joint venture or sale-and-leaseback are not interchangeable. The right structure follows the asset's cash flow, horizon and risk.

03

Infrastructure is location

Power, fibre, cooling, access and regulation increasingly determine what can be built — and what it will be worth when it is finished.

04

Advice should be accountable

We stay through diligence, negotiation and delivery. A recommendation is only useful if it survives contact with the transaction.

03 — APPROACH

A clear route from question to decision.

We scope work in phases so that the mandate can stop, change or accelerate as the evidence becomes clearer.

01

Frame the question

Define the decision, the constraints and what would change our mind. A tight brief prevents an expensive answer to the wrong question.

02

Underwrite the asset

Test demand, income, cost, planning and capital against downside cases — not only the base case that makes the deal work.

03

Structure the route

Match the opportunity to the right capital, partner and transaction shape, with a timetable that reflects how the market actually clears.

04

Deliver and steward

Support diligence, negotiation and handover — then stay close for the next decision the asset requires.

Independent by design.

We are not owned by a developer, a fund or a brokerage, and we do not carry our own inventory. Our income comes from advisory mandates — not from persuading a client to transact.

That independence matters most when the answer is uncomfortable: a site that should not be bought, a rent that will not hold, a capital structure that needs to change before the deal can proceed.
