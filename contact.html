02 — WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

A short, clear first process.

We will not send a brochure and disappear. The first exchange is designed to establish whether there is a real fit.

01

Initial response

Within two business days, a partner reviews your enquiry and replies with either a suggested call or a clear reason why we are not the right fit.

02

Scoping call

A 30-minute conversation on the asset, the decision, the capital and the timetable. No obligation, and no generic presentation.

03

Proposal

If there is a fit, we send a short scope with phases, deliverables, team and fees. You can stop after any phase.

Before you write

Please do not send confidential, commercially sensitive or price-sensitive information in the form or by email before we have agreed a confidentiality undertaking. A short description of the decision you are facing is enough to start.
