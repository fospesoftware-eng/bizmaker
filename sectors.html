03 — THE CROSS-SECTOR LENS

The questions that travel.

Whatever the sector, the same four tests decide whether an asset deserves capital.

01

Location is a system

Access, power, fibre, cooling and planning matter as much as postcode. A site is only as good as the infrastructure that reaches it.

02

Income quality beats headline yield

We look through occupancy to covenant, lease length, escalation, reinstatement and the cost of retaining the tenant at renewal.

03

Exit is part of the entry case

The buyer pool at exit determines the price you can pay today. We underwrite the sale before we underwrite the purchase.

04

Regulation is a value driver

Free-zone status, foreign ownership, licensing and operating rules can change both the income and the universe of buyers.
