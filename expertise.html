02 — EXPERTISE

Three perspectives.
One strategic view.

Advisory, Capital and Infrastructure are not separate service lines. They are three lenses on the same decision — applied together, in the order the asset demands.

PRACTICE 01Advisory — understand the opportunity
PRACTICE 02Capital — connect with capital
PRACTICE 03Infrastructure — future economy

Most mandates begin with a question that does not fit a single discipline.

Should we buy the site, partner on it, or sell it with consent? Is the income durable enough for core capital, or does it need a value-add story? Can the grid connection be secured before the land is priced?

These are not separate questions. They are the same asset examined from different angles. We work across all three practices so the answer holds together.

01

Advisory

Understand the opportunity.

We begin with evidence: what the asset is, what the market will support, and what has to be true for the numbers to work. Our advisory work is built for decision-makers who need a defensible view, not a market brochure.

That means testing the demand case, the planning envelope, the cost to deliver and the exit — then telling you plainly where the risk sits.

  • Market intelligence & research
  • Feasibility & highest-and-best-use
  • Asset & portfolio strategy
  • Development advisory
  • Market entry & investor representation
  • Land & site strategy

Typical mandatesA family office testing its first Dubai acquisition. A developer repositioning a stalled masterplan. An institutional investor comparing three entry routes into the UAE.

Discuss an advisory mandate

02

Capital

Connect opportunity with capital.

A good asset can fail with the wrong capital structure. We translate the investment case into a structure that fits the risk, the horizon and the counterparties — then help carry it through diligence and close.

Our work spans direct acquisitions, joint ventures, club deals and recapitalisations, with an emphasis on alignment: who bears which risk, and what happens when the plan changes.

  • Investment structuring
  • Joint ventures & partnerships
  • Acquisitions & dispositions
  • Capital strategy & introductions
  • Sale & leaseback
  • Exit planning

Typical mandatesA landowner seeking a development partner. A sponsor recapitalising an income asset. An investor comparing direct acquisition with a club deal.

Discuss a capital mandate

03

Infrastructure

Build around the future economy.

Data centres and digital infrastructure are real estate, energy and connectivity at once. We advise on the full stack — site, power, fibre, planning, delivery and the commercial model that makes the asset bankable.

The constraint is rarely land. It is the substation, the cooling capacity, the fibre route and the operator who can make the campus work. We underwrite those before the real estate is priced.

  • Data centre site selection
  • Power & connectivity strategy
  • Strategic land assembly
  • Development management
  • Infrastructure advisory
  • Operator & offtake alignment

Typical mandatesA 30MW campus searching for a credible site. A landowner with a grid connection and no development route. A fund underwriting its first digital infrastructure asset.

Discuss an infrastructure mandate

04 — ENGAGEMENT

How an engagement actually runs.

Work is scoped in phases, with a clear deliverable at the end of each. You can stop after any phase without losing the analysis.

01

Brief

A single conversation to define the decision, the asset, the capital available and the constraints that cannot move.

02

Evidence

Valuation, demand, planning, infrastructure and capital tested against downside cases — with sources and assumptions shown.

03

Structure

A recommendation with a route to execution: partner, capital, transaction shape and a realistic timetable.

04

Execution

Diligence, negotiation and delivery support until the decision is closed — and a clear handover afterwards.

Engagements are partner-led and scoped in phases. We will tell you when a mandate is not a fit — including when the right answer is to wait.
