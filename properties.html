02 — HOW WE PRESENT ASSETS

The information behind the listing.

Every mandate is presented with the same discipline — whether it is a single asset or a platform.

01

The asset case

Location, specification, planning status, condition and the capital required to bring the asset to its intended position.

02

The income case

Covenant, lease profile, occupancy cost, escalation and the realistic renewal or re-letting assumptions behind the yield.

03

The capital case

Entry structure, financing, partner alignment, hold period and the exit route — including who is likely to buy at the other end.

Nothing on this page constitutes an offer, a solicitation or investment advice. Asset details are indicative and subject to change, verification and a signed confidentiality undertaking.
